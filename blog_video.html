Villach Region – Lake Faaker See – Lake Ossiacher See: Nature Adventures & Alpine Charm

Discover Villach, where the Alpine way of life meets Mediterranean flair. Experience crystal-clear lakes, impressive mountain vistas, genuine warmth, and a charming old town in the heart of Carinthia.

I love exploring the Alps and their hidden treasures. From hiking up Mt. Dobratsch to stand-up paddling on Faaker See, the Villach region offers the ideal balance of adventure and relaxation

The Villach region offers the best of both worlds: majestic mountain peaks and crystal-clear lakes with pristine drinking-water quality. Whether you're seeking relaxation by the water or eager to summit the peaks of the Dobratsch Nature Park, your ultimate Alpe-Adria adventure begins here.

Wandering through the historic old town of Villach reveals charming lanes, cozy cafés, and traditional taverns. After exploring, the warm waters of Faaker See or Ossiacher See offer the perfect spot to relax. As evening falls, guests can enjoy regional Alpe-Adria cuisine, starring fresh Carinthian Kasnudeln and local fish.

  1. Pristine swimming lakes:
    Faaker See dazzles with its vivid turquoise waters, while Ossiacher See is perfect for water sports and bike tours.
  2. Dobratsch Nature Park:
    As Carinthia's oldest nature park, it boasts pristine nature, scenic panoramic trails, and breathtaking vistas of the Julian Alps.
  3. Alpine-Adriatic Culinary:
    Discover a unique fusion of Austrian, Italian, and Slovenian flavors in the region's traditional Buschenschenke taverns.

Situated at the border triangle of Austria, Italy, and Slovenia, Villach makes the perfect base for day trips. From unwinding at the Kärnten Therme spa in Warmbad-Villach to biking along the Drau Cycle Path, the region's variety captivates visitors year-round.

Pro tipps

  1. Take advantage of the Villach Region Erlebnis CARD to enjoy free guided tours, bike buses, and activity programs.
